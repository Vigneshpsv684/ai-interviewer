# 🤖 AI Interviewer

An AI-powered interview practice platform that simulates technical interviews using Large Language Models (LLMs). The system generates interview questions, accepts text or voice-based answers, evaluates candidate responses using AI, and provides performance analytics and downloadable interview reports.

The platform also supports resume-based interviews, allowing candidates to upload their resume and practice questions tailored to their skills, projects, and experience.

## 🎯 Project Overview

Preparing for technical interviews often requires repeated practice, but candidates may not always have access to an interviewer who can provide immediate feedback.

The AI Interviewer addresses this problem by providing an interactive interview environment where candidates can:

- Select their target role and experience level
- Choose interview difficulty
- Practice with AI-generated technical questions
- Answer questions using text or voice
- Receive AI-based evaluation and feedback
- Practice interviews based on their resume
- Track previous interview performance
- Analyze score trends
- Download detailed interview reports as PDF

The goal is to provide a realistic and accessible environment for continuous interview preparation.
## ✨ Key Features

### 🧠 AI-Powered Interview Questions
Generates technical interview questions based on the selected role, experience level, and difficulty.

### 🎤 Voice-Based Interview
Allows candidates to interact with the interviewer using speech input and text-to-speech responses.

### 📄 Resume-Based Interview
Candidates can upload their resume and receive personalized interview questions based on their skills and projects.

### 📊 AI Answer Evaluation
Evaluates candidate answers and provides a score, feedback, strengths, and areas for improvement.

### 📈 Performance Analytics
Tracks interview scores and provides statistics such as average score, best score, lowest score, and performance changes.

### 📑 PDF Interview Reports
Generates downloadable PDF reports containing interview performance and question-by-question evaluation.

### 🔐 User Authentication
Provides registration, login, session management, and user-specific interview history.

### 🗂️ Interview History
Stores previous interviews so candidates can review their performance and detailed evaluations.
